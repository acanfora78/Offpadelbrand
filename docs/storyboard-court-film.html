OFF Padel — Technical Storyboard — Rev A

Bro, ma che racchetta è?

Shot list and edit assembly for the POV court film. Seven scenes plus a loop join, cut for TikTok, Reels and Meta Ads.

1080×1920 9:16 30 fps 24.0s / 720 frames H.264 MP4 Assembly: Remotion

Five of seven scenes cannot be built from the current project. The concept is live action — two performers, a court, spoken dialogue, a ball strike. The project holds twelve studio product stills, one logo and one environment photograph. It holds no footage, no location plates and no voice recordings.

The shot list below is written to be shot. Everything marked Build now can be assembled today from the real assets; everything marked Shoot needs a half-day on a court before it can exist.

Wardrobe & props

Talent must wear the actual SKUs. If the footage shows a garment that is not in the catalogue, the film stops selling anything.

  • Protagonist — Performance T-shirt white, shorts black, socks, cap optional. Racket: Obsidian.
  • Friend — Performance T-shirt black, shorts white.
  • Props on the bench — bag, bottle, towel, overgrip box, Pearl as the second racket.
  • Location — the wall in the existing environment photograph is the reference for grade and light: hard afternoon sun, long diagonal shadows, warm concrete.
